RAG faithfulness audit

Does the answer say what the sources say?

A retriever pulls passages for a question; a model writes an answer. This page checks that answer one claim at a time and marks it up the way a fact-checker would: backed by a source, found in no source, or contradicting one. Everything runs here, in your browser.
